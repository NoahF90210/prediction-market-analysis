Methodology and Caveats

The point here is to make the measurement choices legible. That matters at least as much as the charts if you are using this as a portfolio project.

In scope

Resolved binary sports and elections markets only. Multi-outcome contracts, low-volume markets, and unresolved contracts are excluded.

Scoring rule

Brier score is the main accuracy metric, with log loss included as a stricter penalty for overconfident misses. Calibration compares average forecast probability to realized YES rate.

Platform caveat

Polymarket rows in this build are history-based. Kalshi rows are currently snapshot-based fallback forecasts unless historical market history has been fetched and cached.
